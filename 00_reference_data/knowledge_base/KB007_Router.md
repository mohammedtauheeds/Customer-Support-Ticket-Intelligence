# KB007: Router / Device Issue

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Router not working; WiFi range is poor; Router overheating; Forgot WiFi password.

## Resolution Steps

1. Guide a factory reset (hold the reset button for 10 seconds) and set up again.
2. Adapters and routers are replaced free within the 12-month warranty.
3. Offer a mesh extender at Rs 1,499 for large homes.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
