# KB008: New Connection

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: New connection required; Installation pending; When will technician come for installation; Booked new connection 5 days back.

## Resolution Steps

1. New installations are completed within 72 hours of booking where feasible.
2. The installation charge is Rs 1,000, waived on annual plans.
3. Escalate bookings older than 72 hours to the area field supervisor.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
