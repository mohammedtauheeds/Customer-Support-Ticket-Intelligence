# KB009: Disconnection Request

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Cancel my connection; Close my account; Want to disconnect; Permanent disconnection.

## Resolution Steps

1. Attempt retention: offer a relocation check or a plan discount.
2. Router return pickup within 5 days; the security deposit is refunded in 15 days after return.
3. Close the account after the dues are cleared.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
