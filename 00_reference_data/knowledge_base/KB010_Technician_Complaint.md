# KB010: Technician Complaint

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Technician did not come; Technician asked for extra money; Rude behaviour by technician; Visit scheduled but missed.

## Resolution Steps

1. Apologise and reschedule a priority visit.
2. No extra cash should be collected; all charges are on the bill. Report demands for cash to the vigilance team.
3. Log the complaint against the partner ID.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
