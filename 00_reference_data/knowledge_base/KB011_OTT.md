# KB011: OTT / Add-on Services

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: OTT subscription not activated; Bundled app login issue; Free OTT not working.

## Resolution Steps

1. Resend the activation link to the registered mobile.
2. OTT activation takes up to 24 hours after plan activation.
3. If it has expired while the plan is active, re-sync entitlements from the partner portal.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
