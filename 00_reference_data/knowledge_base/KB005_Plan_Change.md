# KB005: Plan Change / Upgrade

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Want to upgrade plan; Change to annual plan; Downgrade request; Which plan is best for work from home.

## Resolution Steps

1. Share the current plan catalogue.
2. Upgrades are effective immediately with pro-rata billing; downgrades apply from the next billing cycle.
3. Annual plans get 2 months free.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
