# KB012: Static IP / Business

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Need static IP; Static IP not working; Business plan port forwarding; CCTV remote view not working.

## Resolution Steps

1. Static IP costs Rs 300 per month and is available on business and home plans of 100 Mbps and above.
2. Check the IP binding on the BNG and the customer router NAT settings.
3. Port forwarding must be configured on the customer router.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
