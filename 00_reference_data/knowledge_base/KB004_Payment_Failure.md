# KB004: Payment Failure

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Payment failed but money debited; UPI payment not updated; Auto-pay failed; Unable to pay bill online.

## Resolution Steps

1. Search the payment gateway log by UTR.
2. If the payment is captured but not posted, post it manually and restore the service within 2 hours.
3. If the payment failed at the bank, the bank auto-reverses in 5-7 days.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
