# KB003: Billing Dispute

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Wrong bill amount; Charged twice; Bill is higher than my plan; Excess charges in invoice.

## Resolution Steps

1. Verify invoice line items in the billing system.
2. Check for pro-rata charges after a plan change, and late fees.
3. For duplicate payments, raise a refund; refunds are processed in 7 working days.
4. Offer a one-time late-fee waiver if paid within 3 days of the due date.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
