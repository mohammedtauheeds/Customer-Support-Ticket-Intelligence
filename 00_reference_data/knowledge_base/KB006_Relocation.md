# KB006: Relocation / Shifting

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: Shifting to new house; Relocation request; Need connection at new address; House shift.

## Resolution Steps

1. Check feasibility at the new address.
2. Relocation fee is Rs 500 within the same city.
3. Schedule the disconnection and installation on the same day where possible.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
