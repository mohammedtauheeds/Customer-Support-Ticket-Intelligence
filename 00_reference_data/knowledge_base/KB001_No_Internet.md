# KB001: No Internet / Connection Down

**Applies to:** All home and business broadband plans
**Last reviewed:** 2025-06-30

## Symptoms

Customers typically report: No internet since morning; Internet not working; Connection down!!; Broadband not working.

## Resolution Steps

1. Check the LOS/PON light on the ONT. A red LOS light means a fibre cut or signal loss.
2. Power cycle the ONT and router for 30 seconds.
3. Check the area outage dashboard; if an outage is active, share the ETR with the customer.
4. If no outage, raise a field visit with SLA 24 hours.

## Customer Communication

Always confirm the customer ID and registered mobile number before sharing account details. Never ask for OTPs or card numbers.
